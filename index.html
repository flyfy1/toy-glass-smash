🔨 Glass Smash: Smash a pane of glass with the word you hate written on it.

A pane of tempered glass with the word you hate on it. Smash it. Ten fast hits and it bursts into slow-motion shards. A tiny stress-relief toy, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. The screen is a pane of tempered glass with an annoying word on it (BUG, MONDAY, DEADLINE and so on, depending on your language).
  2. Click or tap to hit it. Every hit leaves radial cracks, throws glass splinters and plays a heavy glass knock, and the screen shakes.
  3. Hit it 8 to 10 times quickly and the whole pane shatters into about 140 physics shards that fall in slow motion. A fresh pane slides in one second later.
  4. Type your own words in the box at the top, separated by commas: your boss, your bug, your commute. They are added to the page link (for example ?w=BUG,MONDAY), so when you share it your friends get the same words to smash.
  5. Tap Share to send it to friends: the system share sheet on phones, or copy link, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Weibo, Reddit and a QR code on computers.

FAQ

Is Glass Smash free?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up, no download and no ads. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Does it have sound and vibration?

Yes. Every sound is synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to load. On Android phones it also vibrates at the big moments; iPhone browsers do not support web vibration.

Which languages does it support?

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Español, Français and 日本語 (Japanese). The language is picked automatically from your browser settings; any other language falls back to English.

Who made it?

Integ Life (integ.life). Glass Smash is one of five small stress-relief toys, together with Charge Burst, Void Harvest, Chain Pop, Knock on Wood. More games are at games.integ.life.

Is any personal data collected?

No account and no personal data. At most, anonymous usage statistics (page views and play counts) are collected.
